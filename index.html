Weather

Each zone's weather follows a fixed 2,160-day cycle. Every Vana'diel day names a usual, a common and a rare weather, and the game switches among those three during the day, so this is a forecast of odds, not a promise. Double weather is in bold.

From 02:00 to 07:00 Vana'diel time, LandSandBoat servers show Fog here whenever the weather would be Clear, Sunshine or Clouds. Cities don't get this morning fog, and LandSandBoat notes that retail fog works differently.

ffxi-timer v0.5

A Vana'diel clock and schedule board for Final Fantasy XI. Everything is computed in the page from the Earth clock; nothing is fetched and nothing leaves the browser.

What it shows

  • Vana'diel time and date, the weekday's strong and weak elements, day or night, and the next day change
  • Moon percent and phase, with the next phase change, full moon and new moon
  • JP midnight, the weekly conquest tally (Monday 00:00 JST) and this week's RSE race and dungeon
  • All 8 airships, the 6 Selbina, Mhaura, Whitegate and Nashmau boats, the Manaclipper and the Phanauet barge: boarding status, departure in Vana'diel and local time, countdown and landing time
  • Trips from any port to any port boats and airships connect, changing where one lands and the next leaves, such as Port Bastok to Kazham through Jeuno
  • 22 guild shops and 10 other shops with set hours (Tenshodo, linkshells, chips, ferry fishing), with each NPC, holiday, open or closed status and the next open stretch in local time
  • Day, moon and RSE calendars, and a planner that finds when weekday, hour and moon conditions next line up, with saved searches that can each have an alarm
  • Add to calendar: calendar lists and planner windows download as an .ics file for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar, with a reminder
  • A converter between Earth and Vana'diel dates and times, with the weekday, moon and your starred zones' weather
  • RSE details for any week: the chest key to trade, every monster that drops it, your race's set pieces and the sachet NM
  • A weather forecast for 218 zones, and weather as a planner condition; press Star zone to keep a zone's weather for today and tomorrow at the top and in the mini clock
  • A weather finder: click any weather to see which zones are most likely to have it and when
  • Copy link buttons that share a planner search, a zone's forecast or a weather finder search
  • Alarms with browser notifications, a chime and optional Discord posts you can switch per alarm, and a mini clock that stays on top of the game window (Chrome and Edge)
  • A Simple view: the clock, every departure soonest first, the guilds and the next eight days on one screen

Accuracy

Departure times are when the boarding door shuts, from the retail packet captures LandSandBoat records for each run. They sit a few Vana'diel minutes after the round numbers older timers list, so the Jeuno to San d'Oria airship shows 01:14 rather than 01:10. Moon phase names follow the percent ranges BG Wiki lists, which are not symmetric: 43% waning is still Last Quarter and 38% waxing is still a Waxing Crescent. Chest key drops, guild hours, the RSE quest steps and the weather cycles come from LandSandBoat's data and scripts. Weather is a forecast of odds: each day names three weathers and the game moves among them. Outside cities, LandSandBoat servers also turn Clear, Sunshine and Clouds into Fog from 02:00 to 07:00.
