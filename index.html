Weather

The forecast uses a 2,160-day weather table for each zone. Every Vana'diel day names a usual, a common and a rare weather, and the game switches among those three during the day, so this is a forecast of odds, not a promise. Double weather is in bold.

From 02:00 to 07:00 Vana'diel time, LandSandBoat servers show Fog here whenever the weather would be Clear, Sunshine or Clouds. Cities don't get this morning fog, and LandSandBoat notes that retail fog works differently.

Elemental magic

Day and weather raise or lower elemental magic damage. The element's weather adds 10%, or 25% if it is double weather, and its day adds 10%; the opposing element's weather and day take the same off, and the total stops at +40%. Without an obi this happens on about a third of casts. The element's obi makes every cast get the bonuses. Hachirin-no-Obi makes every cast get the bonuses and the penalties.

Notorious monsters

These NMs can only appear at certain hours, under a certain moon or in certain weather. A weather day is a day that weather can come up; the game moves among the day's three weathers, so it may not. A lottery NM takes its placeholder's place by chance when the placeholder respawns after a kill. Press Killed when one dies and its next chance waits for its timer. Point at, tap or focus an NM's name for its level, placeholder, map position and drops. The lottery chances, timers, levels and drop rates are LandSandBoat's.

ffxi-timer v1.0

A Vana'diel clock and schedule board for Final Fantasy XI. Everything is computed in the page from this device's clock, so keep that clock set to update automatically. Nothing is fetched and nothing is sent anywhere, except alarm posts to a Discord webhook if you set one.

What it shows

  • Vana'diel time and date, the weekday's strong and weak elements, day or night, and the next day change
  • Moon percent and phase, with the next phase change, full moon and new moon
  • JP midnight, the weekly conquest tally (Monday 00:00 JST) and this week's RSE race and dungeon
  • All 8 airships, the 6 Selbina, Mhaura, Whitegate and Nashmau boats, the Manaclipper and the Phanauet barge: boarding status, departure in Vana'diel and local time, countdown and landing time
  • Trips from any port to any port boats and airships connect, changing where one lands and the next leaves, such as Port Bastok to Kazham through Jeuno
  • 22 guild shops and 10 other shops with set hours (Tenshodo, linkshells, chips, ferry fishing), with each NPC, open or closed status and the next open stretch in local time; Guild holidays closes each one on its weekly holiday, for servers that keep them
  • Day, moon and RSE calendars, and a planner that finds when weekday, hour, moon and weather conditions next line up, with saved searches that can each have an alarm
  • Add to calendar: calendar lists and planner windows download as an .ics file for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar, with a reminder
  • A converter between Earth and Vana'diel dates and times, with the weekday, moon and your starred zones' weather
  • RSE details for any week: the chest key to trade, every monster that drops it, your race's set pieces and the sachet NM
  • A weather forecast for 218 zones, and weather as a planner condition; press Star zone to keep a zone's weather for today and tomorrow at the top and in the mini clock
  • A weather finder: click any weather to see which zones are most likely to have it and when
  • 41 notorious monsters that only appear at certain hours, under a certain moon or in certain weather: what each one needs, how it pops, and when it can next appear, with an alarm
  • Kill times for timed NMs: press Killed and the NM's next chance waits for its timer
  • A card for each NM: point at, tap or focus its name for its level, the monster it pops from, where BG Wiki places it on the map, and its drops at LandSandBoat's base rates
  • Elemental magic: an element's day and weather effects in a zone, today and for the next 16 days, and when +35% next comes
  • Timers for lockouts such as Dynamis, ENM key items and Assault tags, or any number of hours, with alarms
  • Fishing for 100 fish: where each one bites, the baits and lures that hook it, and when the best fishing moon next comes with rain or a squall at each spot
  • Copy link buttons that share a planner search, a zone's forecast, a weather finder search, an element and zone in Elemental magic, or a fish
  • Alarms with browser notifications, a chime and optional Discord posts you can switch per alarm, and a mini clock that stays on top of the game window (Chrome and Edge)
  • A Simple view: the clock, every departure soonest first, the guilds and the next eight days on one screen
  • Links under the header that jump to each panel, a 12 or 24 hour clock, and system, light, dark and Vana'diel themes
  • Several open tabs share their settings and alarms, and one of them delivers each alarm
  • Server rules next to Full and Simple: Retail, HorizonXI or another LandSandBoat server (LSB). They set guild holidays, the Dynamis wait, which timers are offered and the obi note to that server's rules, where those are confirmed

Accuracy

Departure times are when the boarding door shuts, from the retail packet captures LandSandBoat records for each run. They sit a few Vana'diel minutes after the round numbers older timers list, so the Jeuno to San d'Oria airship shows 01:14 rather than 01:10. Moon phase names follow the percent ranges BG Wiki lists, which are not symmetric: 43% waning is still Last Quarter and 38% waxing is still a Waxing Crescent. Chest key drops, guild hours, the RSE quest steps and the weather tables come from LandSandBoat's data and scripts. Times and schedules are the same on every server. Retail dropped guild holidays in June 2014, so with Retail picked guilds are open every day; on HorizonXI and other LandSandBoat servers it is a server setting, so Guild holidays turns them on. NM conditions, timers and drops, and fishing, follow LandSandBoat's rules whichever server is picked, and the panels say so where that server differs. Weather is a forecast of odds: each day names three weathers and the game moves among them. Outside cities, LandSandBoat servers also turn Clear, Sunshine and Clouds into Fog from 02:00 to 07:00.
